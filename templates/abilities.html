Core Abilities

VexoStream bridges the gap between restricted media and your browser utilizing modern edge-proxy techniques.

No VPN Required

Our backend handles the IP routing and proxy networking. Your device connects only to us, completely sidestepping local ISP blocks and DNS filters.

Adaptive Playback

We extract dynamic HLS (.m3u8) manifests and proxy chunk requests on the fly. The built-in HLS.js engine ensures smooth video scaling based on your network speed.

Multi-CDN Scraper

Integrated fallback algorithms extract direct MP4 lines and streams from major providers including Pornhub, XNXX, and XVideos securely.

Zero Logging

Streams are proxied directly in memory. Data is passed through directly to your browser buffer without being written to disk or recorded in databases.

Header Spoofing

The backend bypasses Web Application Firewalls (WAF) by automatically injecting required Origin and Referer headers specific to the requested target.

Railway Optimized

Built and containerized natively for Railway's edge architecture, ensuring high bandwidth throughput directly from US datacenters.
