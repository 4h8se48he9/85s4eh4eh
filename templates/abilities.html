System Architecture

VexoStream bridges the gap between restricted CDNs and your local network utilizing modern edge-proxy evasion techniques.

Zero-Config VPN

The backend handles routing and proxy networking. Your device connects only to the gateway, sidestepping ISP blocks completely.

YouTube Support

Full support for YouTube infrastructure. The scraper executes JS runtimes (Deno) automatically to decrypt signatures and extract feeds.

Adaptive HLS

Extracts dynamic HLS (.m3u8) manifests and rewrites chunk URIs on the fly. The built-in engine scales resolution automatically.

In-Memory Tunnel

Streams are passed through directly via byte-range requests. Data hits your browser without touching the server's disk.

WAF Bypass

Identifies the requested CDN and dynamically injects precise Origin and Referer headers to bypass strict hotlink protections.

Cloud Native

Built as a stateless WSGI application. Optimized for Railway Nixpacks to ensure maximum upstream bandwidth utilization.
